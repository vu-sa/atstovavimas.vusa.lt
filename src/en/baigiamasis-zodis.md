# Concluding Remarks

Methodological publication: "Representation Guide," Vilnius: Vilnius
University Student Representation, 2021.

The information presented in the methodological publication
"Representation Guide" consists of two parts. The first part describes
and illustrates the structure of higher education policy and related
structures, which will help student representatives better understand
which parties to contact and which issues to address. It also presents
the processes for initiating legislation and adopting government
resolutions. The second part contains systematic practical information
that student representatives need when operating at the national level.
This interactive electronic publication will be updated with the most
relevant practical information for student representative organizations,
student representatives, and those interested in higher education. The
publication can be used as a teaching tool.

![EU Programme](/img/ESFIVP.jpg){height="100"}

The "Representation Guide" is part of the project ["Improving Public
Management in Education by Involving Non-Governmental
Organizations"](https://vusa.lt/lt/viesojo-valdymo-svietimo-srityje-gerinimas-itraukiant-nevyriausybines-organizacijas-).
The project is financed by the European Union Structural Funds.
