# Organizations Representing Students at the National Level

## VU SR

Vilnius University Students' Representation (VU SR) -- the oldest and
largest non-profit, non-political, expert educational organization in
Lithuania, representing the interests of students at Vilnius University
and beyond, fostering university culture and creating the most favorable
conditions for student self-expression.

VU SR is the only students' representation that represents the interests
of its students at the national level.

[More about VU SR activities](https://vusa.lt/lt)

## **The Lithuanian National Union of Students**

The Lithuanian National Union of Students (LSS) is a national,
democratic, independent and non-governmental organization representing
the interests of Lithuanian students and uniting the student
self-governments of Lithuanian higher education institutions.

The Lithuanian National Union of Students represents Lithuanian students
at the national level. To fulfill its mission, the Union organizes
events and campaigns.

[More about LSS activities](http://www.lss.lt/veikla-lietuvoje/)
